The Problem: One Student. A Systemic Wall.

Works: Full-time, Mon–Fri 8:00–16:30

Part 3 · The Roadblock 20 points
Part 3 · The Roadblock — 20 pts

Three Findings. All Three Addressed.

Pilot feedback from Renee's persona. Rubric requires at least two addressed. We addressed all three — by modifying the existing build, not starting over.

🕐 Finding 1 — "This assumes I can attend full time"
The problem: The original hero stat showed "~2 semesters (full-time)" — meaningless for someone who works Mon–Fri 8–4:30.
What we changed:
  • Hero stat now shows part-time semesters (2 courses) as the primary number
  • Finding 1 title: "This Plan Works Around Your Job — Not the Other Way Around"
  • Amber alert at top: explicitly states online/evening only, no daytime in-person required
  • Full-time path shown as not recommended given Renee's schedule
  • Spring 2027 schedule table shows ✓/✗ for each section vs. work hours
🏁 Finding 2 — "Show me the shortest path to finishing"
The problem: Finding 2 was collapsed by default and buried below the schedule table. The certificate path wasn't prominent.
What we changed:
  • Finding 2 now opens by default alongside Findings 1 and 3
  • Dynamic headline: "Renee Already Qualifies for the Business Operations Certificate"
  • Green success alert leads: "Renee can earn a credential this semester"
  • Each credential shown as a card sorted by % complete — 100% at the top
  • Explicitly states: every credit stacks into the AAS with zero duplication
🔒 Finding 3 — "I have a hold on my account"
The problem: Holds existed in the data but weren't prominent enough — a student might miss them before trying to register.
What we changed:
  • Danger alert now leads with "Your credits are safe" — directly addresses the fear
  • Both holds surfaced on the Transcript screen before the student even runs the audit
  • Step-by-step "Clear in this order" box: readmission form first (free, 5 min), then Bursar
  • Specifically calls out the Returning Student Balance Forgiveness Program — don't pay first
  • Closes: advisors can start the credit evaluation before holds are cleared
🎙 Talk Track (~40 sec) Three findings came back from the pilot. Finding 1: the plan assumed full-time attendance — Renee works. We made part-time the headline number and flagged every section against her work hours. Finding 2: show the shortest path — we made Finding 2 open by default with a green banner that says "Renee can earn a credential this semester." Finding 3: the holds — we surfaced both on the transcript screen before she even runs the audit, with a step-by-step clear order and a note to ask about the Balance Forgiveness Program before paying.

2030 Impact Statement — Key Points

Who benefits

Every Ivy Tech stop-out student — particularly working adults, single parents, and low-income learners who can't afford the time cost of the old appointment-dependent process.

Which metric moves, by how much

Stop-out credential completion rate. Target: 1,000/year by 2030. Tool removes the decision barrier — the moment Renee thinks "I don't know where to start" and closes the tab.

What it takes to roll out across Ivy Tech

SIS integration to pull real transcripts · Program catalog data feed · A-IVY advising layer · Proactive outreach to stopped-out students via text

The one risk we'd watch

Over-reliance on self-service: a student who sees "100% done" may not complete the formal readmission steps. Mitigation: every screen surfaces a human advisor path and the holds check.
