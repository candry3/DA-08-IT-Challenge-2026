The Four Strategic Layers
🧑‍💼 Persona — Jordan Rivera
A 34-year-old warehouse supervisor in Indianapolis. Single parent. Stopped out of Ivy Tech in 2016 with 58 credits after a family health crisis. Today, a promotion requires a credential — one they've essentially already earned, they just don't know it yet.

"Tell me what I already have and show me what's left. Don't make me start over."

🏛️ Pillar — Ivy Tech Strategic Plan 2030: Student-Centered Journey
This pillar targets stop-out students specifically — removing friction from re-enrollment, making credit recognition seamless, and meeting adult learners where they are. Jordan's story is not an edge case; it is the center of this pillar.

💡 Solution — Credit Audit Dashboard: "Come Back and Finish"
One session. No advisor appointment needed. Three plain-language answers:

Question	Section
What credits still count?	✅ Credits That Apply
What credits no longer fit?	⚠️ No Longer Count
What do I still need?	📋 Still Needed
Design principle: The student should be able to answer "Am I closer than I thought?" before they close the tab.

📊 Measurable 2030 Metric
Stage	What We Measure	Target
Prototype	Demo viewers who ask a re-enrollment follow-up	> 60%
Pilot Year 1	Stop-out students who begin an application after using the tool	25%
2030	Stop-out students who complete a credential within 24 months of return	1,000 / year
