# Impact Statement — *Come Back and Finish*
### A Credit Audit Tool for Returning Adult Learners at Ivy Tech Community College

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Indiana has tens of thousands of Hoosiers who started college, earned real credits, and stopped out before finishing — not because they failed, but because life intervened. For these students, the path back has been defined by **uncertainty and friction**: unclear credit status, mandatory advisor appointments, and a process designed for first-time enrollees — not working adults, single parents, or people whose employers are now requiring a credential.

**The uncertainty is the wall.** Most stop-out students never make it past it.

---

## What This Tool Does

**Come Back and Finish** is a self-service Credit Audit Dashboard that answers the three questions every returning student asks — in a single session, without an appointment:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| *"What credits do I already have that count toward my degree today?"* | ✅ Credits That Apply |
| *"What credits I took no longer fit the program?"* | ⚠️ Credits That No Longer Count |
| *"How close am I to finishing?"* | 📋 Remaining Requirements + Progress Bar |

A student like **Jordan Rivera** — a 34-year-old warehouse supervisor, single parent, 58 credits earned, stopped out in 2016 — can visit this tool and know, before they close the tab, that they are closer to the finish line than they thought.

---

## The Measurable Impact

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Prototype | Demo viewers who ask a re-enrollment follow-up question | > 60% |
| Pilot — Year 1 | Stop-out students who begin an application after using the tool | 25% conversion rate |
| **2030 Goal** | **Stop-out students who complete a credential after returning** | **1,000 / year** |

Every credential earned by a returning student who came back because they finally understood their progress is a counted outcome under **Ivy Tech's 2030 completion goals**.

---

## Strategic Alignment

This project directly operationalizes **Pillar 3: Student-Centered Journey** from Ivy Tech's Strategic Plan 2030, which commits to:

- Streamlining enrollment and advising processes
- Establishing transparent success roadmaps
- Building student momentum
- Delivering seamless, unified experiences at every stage of the student lifecycle

Stop-out students are not strangers to Ivy Tech — they are Ivy Tech students who left and deserve a clear, respectful path back. This tool is what that path looks like in practice.

The tool also advances **Pillar 2: Career & Transfer Pathways** by connecting credential completion directly to workforce outcomes — such as the employer-required promotion Jordan has already earned on the floor but cannot receive without the degree.

---

## Why It Matters Beyond One Student

- **Scale:** Ivy Tech serves over 160,000 students annually across Indiana. The stop-out population represents a large, undercounted pipeline of adults whose partially-completed credentials are stranded.
- **Equity:** The students most likely to stop out — working adults, parents, low-income learners — are the least likely to have time to navigate a slow, appointment-dependent re-enrollment process. This tool removes that inequity by design.
- **Efficiency:** Advisors are a finite resource. Every stop-out student who can self-answer *"Am I close enough to come back?"* is an appointment saved — and an advisor freed to serve students with more complex needs.
- **Economic Impact:** Each credential completion has downstream workforce and wage effects for the student, their family, and Indiana's talent pipeline.

---

## The Design Principle

> *"Tell me what I already have and show me what's left. Don't make me start over."*

This tool was designed from that single sentence. The student's time, trust, and existing work are respected from the first screen to the last. That is the impact.
