# 2030 Impact Statement
## Come Back and Finish — Credit Audit Planner
**Ivy Tech IT Challenge 2026 — Case DA-08**

---

## The Problem We Solved

Hundreds of thousands of Ivy Tech students have stopped out over the years — many of them adults who left with real, earned credits but no clear picture of how those credits apply today. Without a fast, self-service way to see where they stand, the re-enrollment decision feels overwhelming. The result: they don't come back.

---

## What We Built

A **"Come Back and Finish" credit audit planner** — a single-page web tool that lets a returning adult student:

1. See their past credits mapped against a current Ivy Tech degree program
2. Instantly know which credits still apply, which don't, and exactly what remains
3. Walk away with a clear, actionable roadmap — not a 90-minute advisor wait

The tool runs in any browser, requires no login, and uses plain language designed for a busy adult learner, not an academic registrar.

## A-Ivy Principles

This solution was designed in alignment with Ivy Tech's A-Ivy responsible AI principles:

- **AI supports people, it does not replace them** — the tool ends with a clear footer directing students to a human advisor for their official evaluation
- **Answers grounded in approved sources** — all credit matching is based on the official program requirement data, not AI inference
- **Users always have a path to a human** — every page state surfaces the advisor referral
- **Personal data is protected** — the prototype uses no real student data; a production version would operate within Ivy Tech's existing data governance framework
- **A person remains in charge of high-impact decisions** — the tool informs; the student and advisor decide
- **Designed for accessibility** — semantic HTML, ARIA roles on the progress bar, responsive layout
